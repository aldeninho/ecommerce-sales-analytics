# Business insights and recommendations

## Executive summary

The business generated **$2.22M in net revenue** and **$716.2K in gross profit** across 5,200 orders. Gross margin was **32.2%** and completed-order AOV was **$476.74**. Returns and cancellations removed **$281.7K** from booked revenue, equal to **11.2%** of potential revenue.

## Findings

1. **Furniture and Electronics produced 73.5% of net revenue.** Furniture led with $821.6K and Electronics generated $813.2K. Protect inventory availability and marketing visibility for the best products in these categories, while tracking margin and return risk alongside sales.

2. **Furniture created the largest revenue-recovery opportunity.** The category lost $135.6K, or 48.1% of all lost revenue, and had a 14.2% lost-revenue rate. Review product quality, damage-in-transit, product descriptions, and return reasons before increasing Furniture promotion.

3. **Regional performance diverged sharply in 2025.** South revenue grew 16.0% and West grew 2.9%, while North declined 3.2% and Central declined 25.3%. Investigate Central by category, customer segment, and order status, then test whether South's channel and product mix can be replicated.

4. **Overall 2025 revenue grew approximately 0.7% despite strong growth in South.** Growth in South and West was not enough to offset Central's decline. Regional action plans should focus on recovery in Central rather than treating the company-wide result as a broad demand problem.

5. **Returns caused more operational pressure than cancellations.** The return rate was 7.0% compared with a 3.3% cancellation rate. A return-reduction program should focus first on Furniture, while cancellation analysis should examine fulfillment capacity and payment failure by region.

6. **The largest monthly decline occurred in April 2025.** Net revenue fell 17.8% month over month to $89.1K. August 2025 also fell 11.8% to $87.5K. Add drill-through from each monthly point to region, category, and order status so managers can distinguish seasonality from execution problems.

7. **Revenue is broadly distributed across customers.** The ten largest customers contributed only 7.6% of total net revenue, and the top customer contributed 0.9%. The company has limited single-customer dependency, so retention programs should target valuable customer cohorts rather than rely on a few individual accounts.

## Recommended dashboard actions

- Create an alert for regions with negative YoY growth and categories with lost-revenue rates above 12%.
- Add a drill-through page for returned and cancelled orders.
- Review the Central region monthly and compare its product mix with South.
- Track Furniture return reasons and delivery damage before expanding promotions.
- Monitor AOV together with gross margin so discount-led growth does not reduce profitability.
